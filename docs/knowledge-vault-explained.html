Knowledge-Vault

An AI engine that writes study material for any syllabus. We type the requirements; it researches the web, checks its facts, and returns an exam-ready study pack.

01

What we are building

Seedhi baat

Hum notes bechne wali company nahi, woh machine bana rahe hain jo kisi bhi university ke syllabus ke best notes khud bana de. Hamari 5 hand-made e-books uska gold standard hain.

02

Why it matters

STUDENTS

Scattered notes

Google, YouTube, Telegram PDFs. Not syllabus-wise, often wrong, no idea what scores.

CREATORS

Too slow by hand

One good subject book takes 2–3 days. Covering every university is impossible.

OUR ANSWER

Automate it

One subject in under 30 minutes: syllabus-exact, verified, ★ marked, interactive.

03

The tech: Agentic RAG

BRAIN

LLM

Reads, writes and reasons. Alone, it can make facts up.

TRUTH

RAG

Find real sources first, then write only from them, with citations.

HANDS

Agents

LLMs that use tools (search, reader, Python) and decide their own next step.

04

The pipeline: six AI agents

Think of a publishing house where every employee is an agent. Select a stage to see its job.
